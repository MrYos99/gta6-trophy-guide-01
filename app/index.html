        Sources & honesty notes

        • Guide facts: GTABase — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Achievements List (PS5 & Xbox Series X|S), November 2026.
        • Cross-checked against: PSNProfiles — Grand Theft Auto VI Trophies; Push Square — GTA 6: Trophy Guide & Roadmap.
        • Unconfirmed: “Bad Luck, I Guess” and “Ten Pin” have no confirmed trigger in any source. This guide lists them as unconfirmed and gives no steps for them.
        • Partial: “Fair Game”, “In The Beginning…”, “Capital Offense” and “The Truth Is Out There” have a known direction but no confirmed exact steps — the guide says so on each card.
        • Missable: exactly one trophy can be missed — “Positive Mental Attitude”. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation with Lucia the morning after Down to Earth.
        • Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off your trophy run. There are no online or DLC trophies; one playthrough is enough.
